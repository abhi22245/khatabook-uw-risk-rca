Why this isn't a seasoning artifact

A fair objection: every cohort is measured at the same 2026-09-01 snapshot, so July loans have barely two months of performance while January loans have eight. If young loans simply score higher, the whole trend is maturity, not risk. The spread between Fresh and Renewal answers it — both cohorts sit in the same month with the same seasoning, so anything that moves the gap is real.

Inside the 500: not a few bad loans

The obvious hope is that 7.35% is a handful of blow-ups dragging an otherwise fine book. It isn't — and the proof is not the theoretical even-loss line, which no real credit book sits on, but the healthy book beside it. The OVERRULE-B experiment has more than double the loss rate and the lowest concentration of the three. You cannot fix it by removing a tail, because there is no tail to remove: the book is worse all the way through.

Two separate things are wrong, and they compound. Mix: 54% of this experiment comes from the 4–10 prior-DPD band against 17% for the rest of Renewal, and almost none of it is clean-history (1 loan out of 500, versus 12.4%). Two lenders put through nearly three-quarters of the 500: VIVRITI 203 loans (40.6%) and Western Capital 158 (31.6%). But volume is the less interesting half. So did the OVERRULE-B experiment cause July? No.

Everything above shows the OVERRULE-B experiment is lossy. None of it shows it changed in July — and that is what would make it the cause rather than a standing problem. Tracking it monthly back to April 2025 settles it: the experiment accounts for about 6% of Renewal's June→July move. The rest is lenders — but mostly not the ones that are new.

The largest single component is the one with no name attached to it. Every continuing lender got worse in the same month — VIVRITI 3.10→3.70, SLICE 2.59→3.19, SMICC 2.94→3.11, JUPITER 3.32→3.63, CASHTREE 3.76→6.41. A simultaneous rise across unrelated counterparties is not a lender problem and not an experiment problem; it points upstream of both. That is unexplained and is now the open question.

Volume moved at the same time

The window ran at 427 disbursals a day against June's 333 — a 28% lift in run rate — with every cohort growing. Renewal's monthly count went 3,138 → 4,174 and Dormant's 1,919 → 2,695, both roughly +37% into July. The section above shows where that extra volume came from on the Renewal side: a fifth of it was written by lenders that were barely present in June.

What to hold against these numbers

The deployed table disagrees

uw_decision_monitoring.M0_ECL in production is still on the old source (RISK_M0_MODEL_ECL_PREDICTIONS × 0.88). Only 28% of loans match ECL_PORTFOLIO, and deployed values run ~16% low. Every figure here reads LOAN_ECL_METRICS directly. Don't mix the two.

The ECL snapshot is still filling

The 2026-09-01 BOM grew from 390,223 rows / 320,598 loans on 11 Sep to 530,648 / 321,971 by 15 Sep — the duplicated loans went from 3 rows each to 7. The QUALIFY dedup absorbed it (window figures re-run identical), but older vintages shifted slightly. Re-run before quoting, and expect small drift.

August is ten days

The window ends 10 August, so the August column is a third of a month — 3,842 loans. Treat its rates as directional, not as a monthly print.

Seasoning is uneven

All ECL comes from one BOM (2026-09-01), so July loans carry ~2 months of performance and January loans ~8. Cross-month comparisons of a single cohort inherit that; the Fresh−Renewal spread above is the control.

Dormant is the dashboard definition

is_dormant_flag here is the BRE input flag with a whitelist fallback, which is not the live routing dormancy (flag and isFresh). The two disagree for some users — see WARN_dormant_flag_definition.

AA is carved out first

Cohorts are assigned in order AA → Dormant → Renewal → Fresh, so each loan lands in exactly one and the four sum to the total. Leaving AA in inflates the other three.

Triplicate ECL rows

The 2026-09-01 snapshot holds 390,223 rows for 320,598 loans — 34,763 loans appear three times with identical ECL. The QUALIFY dedup is load-bearing; without it their loss is counted three times.

Verify it yourself

Every number above comes from these queries, unmodified. They are committed to the repo under queries/ on branch abhi_rca. Q1 reproduces the cohort table; Q2 the monthly history behind the first two charts; Q5 the experiment-type split; Q6 the loan-level detail behind the concentration curve, also exported to data/renewal_overrule_bucket_b_500_loans.csv; Q3 and Q4 are the two data-quality checks called out in the caveats.

Q1 — Cohort-wise disbursals + ECL, 1 Jul – 10 Aug 2026 
WITH ecl AS (
    SELECT a.LOAN_ID, a.ECL_PORTFOLIO AS m0_ecl
    FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS a
    WHERE a.BOM = (SELECT MAX(BOM) FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS)
    QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY a.LOAN_ID ORDER BY a.BOM DESC) = 1
),
uw AS (   -- allocated arm only, one row per loan (its latest policy run)
    SELECT LOAN_ID, IS_FRESH, IS_RENEWAL, IS_DORMANT_FLAG, COMBINATION_TYPE
    FROM analytics.model.uw_decision_monitoring
    WHERE MODEL_VERSION = MODEL_VERSION_FINAL
      AND LOAN_ID IS NOT NULL
    QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY LOAN_ID ORDER BY POLICY_RUN_DATE DESC) = 1
),
base AS (
    SELECT
        loc.LOAN_ID, loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE, loc.LOAN_AMOUNT,
        CASE
            WHEN uw.LOAN_ID IS NULL         THEN 'Unmapped'
            WHEN uw.COMBINATION_TYPE = 'AA' THEN 'AA'
            WHEN uw.IS_DORMANT_FLAG = 1     THEN 'Dormant'
            WHEN uw.IS_RENEWAL = 1          THEN 'Renewal'
            ELSE 'Fresh'
        END AS cohort,
        e.m0_ecl
    FROM analytics.model.loan_origination_characteristics loc
    LEFT JOIN uw  ON loc.LOAN_ID = uw.LOAN_ID
    LEFT JOIN ecl e ON loc.LOAN_ID = e.LOAN_ID
    -- half-open: LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE may carry a time component, so
    -- BETWEEN ... AND '2026-08-10' would silently drop most of 10 Aug.
    WHERE loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE >= '2026-07-01'
      AND loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE <  '2026-08-11'
)
SELECT
    cohort,
    COUNT(*)                                   AS loans,
    ROUND(SUM(LOAN_AMOUNT) / 1e7, 2)           AS disbursed_cr,
    ROUND(AVG(LOAN_AMOUNT), 0)                 AS avg_ticket_inr,
    COUNT(m0_ecl)                              AS loans_with_ecl,
    ROUND(COUNT(m0_ecl) * 100.0 / COUNT(*), 1) AS ecl_coverage_pct,
    ROUND(SUM(m0_ecl) / 1e7, 2)                AS ecl_cr,
    ROUND(SUM(m0_ecl) * 100.0
          / NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN m0_ecl IS NOT NULL THEN LOAN_AMOUNT END), 0), 2) AS ecl_pct
FROM base
GROUP BY ROLLUP(cohort)
ORDER BY (cohort IS NULL), loans DESC;
Q2 — Monthly cohort ECL history, Jan – 10 Aug 2026 
WITH ecl AS (
    SELECT a.LOAN_ID, a.ECL_PORTFOLIO AS m0_ecl
    FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS a
    WHERE a.BOM = (SELECT MAX(BOM) FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS)
    QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY a.LOAN_ID ORDER BY a.BOM DESC) = 1
),
uw AS (
    SELECT LOAN_ID, IS_RENEWAL, IS_DORMANT_FLAG, COMBINATION_TYPE
    FROM analytics.model.uw_decision_monitoring
    WHERE MODEL_VERSION = MODEL_VERSION_FINAL AND LOAN_ID IS NOT NULL
    QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY LOAN_ID ORDER BY POLICY_RUN_DATE DESC) = 1
)
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE)::DATE AS disb_month,
       CASE WHEN uw.LOAN_ID IS NULL             THEN 'Unmapped'
            WHEN uw.COMBINATION_TYPE = 'AA'     THEN 'AA'
            WHEN uw.IS_DORMANT_FLAG = 1         THEN 'Dormant'
            WHEN uw.IS_RENEWAL = 1              THEN 'Renewal'
            ELSE 'Fresh' END AS cohort,
       COUNT(*) AS loans,
       ROUND(SUM(loc.LOAN_AMOUNT)/1e7,2) AS disbursed_cr,
       ROUND(SUM(e.m0_ecl)*100.0
             / NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN e.m0_ecl IS NOT NULL THEN loc.LOAN_AMOUNT END),0),2) AS ecl_pct
FROM analytics.model.loan_origination_characteristics loc
LEFT JOIN uw  ON loc.LOAN_ID = uw.LOAN_ID
LEFT JOIN ecl e ON loc.LOAN_ID = e.LOAN_ID
WHERE loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE >= '2026-01-01'
  AND loc.LOAN_DISBURSED_DATE <  '2026-08-11'
GROUP BY 1,2 ORDER BY 1,2;

),
uw AS (
    SELECT LOAN_ID, IS_FRESH, IS_RENEWAL, IS_DORMANT_FLAG, COMBINATION_TYPE

SELECT
    cohort,
    EXPERIMENT_TYPE                  AS experiment_type,
    COUNT(*)                         AS loans,
    ROUND(SUM(m0_ecl) / 1e7, 3)      AS ecl_cr,
    ROUND(SUM(LOAN_AMOUNT) / 1e7, 2) AS disbursed_cr,
    ROUND(SUM(m0_ecl) * 100.0 / NULLIF(SUM(LOAN_AMOUNT), 0), 2) AS ecl_pct,
    -- rupees of loss above what this experiment would carry at the cohort rate
    ROUND((SUM(m0_ecl) - SUM(LOAN_AMOUNT)
           * (SUM(SUM(m0_ecl))      OVER (PARTITION BY cohort)
            / SUM(SUM(LOAN_AMOUNT)) OVER (PARTITION BY cohort))) / 1e7, 3) AS excess_ecl_cr
FROM base
GROUP BY cohort, EXPERIMENT_TYPE
ORDER BY cohort, excess_ecl_cr DESC;
Q3 — Is the deployed table on the new ECL source? 
-- Expectation if the dbt change is live: matches_ecl_portfolio = loans_compared.
-- Observed 2026-09-11: 44,243 of 158,970 (27.8%) — it is NOT live.
SELECT COUNT(*) AS loans_compared,
       SUM(CASE WHEN ABS(dm.M0_ECL - lem.ECL_PORTFOLIO) < 0.01 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS matches_ecl_portfolio,
       SUM(CASE WHEN ABS(dm.M0_ECL - lem.M0_ECL)        < 0.01 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS matches_lem_m0_ecl,
       ROUND(AVG(dm.M0_ECL),2)          AS avg_deployed,
       ROUND(AVG(lem.ECL_PORTFOLIO),2)  AS avg_ecl_portfolio,
       ROUND(AVG(lem.M0_ECL),2)         AS avg_lem_m0_ecl
FROM analytics.model.uw_decision_monitoring dm
JOIN (
  SELECT LOAN_ID, ECL_PORTFOLIO, M0_ECL
  FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS
  WHERE BOM = (SELECT MAX(BOM) FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS)
  QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY LOAN_ID ORDER BY BOM DESC) = 1
) lem ON dm.LOAN_ID = lem.LOAN_ID
WHERE dm.MODEL_VERSION = dm.MODEL_VERSION_FINAL AND dm.M0_ECL IS NOT NULL;
Q4 — Duplicate-row check on the latest BOM snapshot 
-- Observed 2026-09-11 on BOM 2026-09-01:
--   1 row  -> 285,736 loans
--   2 rows ->      99 loans
--   3 rows ->  34,763 loans   <- exact triplicates, identical ECL
-- Only 3 loans in the whole snapshot have differing ECL across their rows,
-- so QUALIFY ... = 1 is safe AND required.
WITH d AS (
  SELECT loan_id, COUNT(*) AS n,
         COUNT(DISTINCT period) AS periods,
         COUNT(DISTINCT mob)    AS mobs,
         COUNT(DISTINCT ecl_portfolio) AS distinct_ecl
  FROM analytics.MODEL.LOAN_ECL_METRICS
  WHERE bom = '2026-09-01'
  GROUP BY 1
)
SELECT n AS rows_per_loan, COUNT(*) AS loans,
       SUM(CASE WHEN distinct_ecl > 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS loans_with_differing_ecl,
       MAX(periods) AS max_distinct_period, MAX(mobs) AS max_distinct_mob
FROM d GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
